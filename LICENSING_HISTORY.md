# USB AI licensing history

This record identifies USB AI source and release assets made available under
the MIT License before the change to the USB AI Personal Use License v1.0. The
MIT text is preserved in [LICENSE-MIT-LEGACY](LICENSE-MIT-LEGACY). Previously
granted MIT rights, including commercial use, remain in effect for those copies.

## Source baseline

The USB AI source through commit
[`4cf59ef7709235092ef3ceba74aab680a3001d4e`](https://github.com/fipra/usb-ai/tree/4cf59ef7709235092ef3ceba74aab680a3001d4e)
was published with the MIT License. This includes the former Electron edition
and the Rust edition present at that commit. Later repository snapshots may
contain changes under different terms; consult the license accompanying each
snapshot. The license for a new distribution cannot cancel the MIT rights in
an earlier copy.
